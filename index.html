Privacy Policy

Version 2  ·  Last updated: 9 October 2026  ·  Applies to the Attendo Android app

Attendo ("we", "us", "the app") is developed and operated by Samundar Singh, Kota, Rajasthan, India (contact: sachoratechnologies@gmail.com). It is an attendance, field-staff and payroll app. Companies (employers) use it to manage attendance and staff, and their employees use it to punch in and out, record trips, apply for leave and see their salary slips.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, how it is stored and shared, and the choices and rights you have. By using Attendo you agree to this policy.

Attendo is used by a company for its own staff. That company (your employer) decides how the attendance and payroll information of its employees is used. For employee data, the employer is the data controller and Attendo processes the data on the employer's behalf.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take the selfie for punch-in / punch-out and service calls. Only the camera is allowed (no gallery), so the photo is live.
  • Location: to record where you punch in / out, attend a call, or start / stop a trip. During an active trip, location is used in the background with a visible notification, and it stops when the trip stops.
  • Notifications: to show the "Trip in progress" notification.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: to unlock the app with your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. This check is done entirely by your phone. Attendo never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data. The app-lock on/off and auto-lock time are saved on the phone and apply to whoever is logged in on it. Logging out does not change them; Delete Account resets them.

You can change permissions anytime in your phone's Settings. Some features, such as punch-in, will not work without them.

3. Offline use and data on your phone

  • When there is no internet, the app shows the last saved data. Actions such as punch-in are stored on your phone and are sent to the server automatically when the internet returns.
  • Without internet, the location used is the phone's last known location, or "nil" if none is available.
  • The full-size attendance photo is saved in the app's own folder on your phone (Android/data/…/Attendo Photos). A small, compressed copy is uploaded so that your employer can verify attendance.
  • The saved screen data (cache) is cleared when you log out. Uninstalling the app removes all app data from the phone.

4. Who can see your information

  • Employees can see only their own attendance, trips, leave, call orders, salary slips and feedback, plus their company's notices and company profile.
  • Employers and approved managers can see the records of employees of their own company: attendance with selfie and location, trips, leave, call orders, salary slips and feedback from their staff.
  • Other companies cannot see your company's data. Access rules are enforced on the server (row-level security).

Service providers

  • Supabase: secure cloud database, login and photo storage.
  • Brevo: sends account emails such as password-reset codes.
  • Android / Google location and geocoding services: provide GPS position and convert coordinates into an address.
  • Google Maps: opens when you tap a map link (governed by Google's own privacy policy).

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. How long we keep data

Data is kept while the account or company is active, so that attendance and salary history is available to the company. When an account is deleted, its data is deleted as described below. Backups made by our hosting provider are removed in their normal backup cycle.

6. Deleting your account

  • Open the app, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm.
  • Employee / manager: your account and your attendance, trips, leave, office requests and call-order records are deleted.
  • Employer: the whole company is deleted, including all employee and manager accounts of that company and all company records.

Deletion cannot be undone. You can also ask for deletion by email (see Contact).

7. Security

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Server-side access rules limit each user to their own or their company's data.
  • Optional app lock with fingerprint / PIN, plus auto-lock after a set time in the background.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your rights

You can access and correct your information in the app or through your employer, delete your account, and withdraw permissions at any time. Where applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, gives you more rights, you can contact us to use them.

9. Children

Attendo is a workplace app and is not meant for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
